Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a PDF to grayscale?

Upload your PDF, open the Grayscale tab and click Convert to Grayscale. Every page is re-rendered in black and white — ideal for printing without colour ink.

What does flattening a PDF do?

Flattening merges fillable form fields into the page content, so filled-in values can no longer be edited or accidentally cleared.

Can I resize a PDF to A4 or Letter?

Yes. Pick A4, Letter, Legal, A5 or a custom size. Keep content position to preserve vector text, or scale content to fit to fill the new size exactly.

How do I add page numbers to every page?

Use the Header/Footer tab and type "Page {page} of {total}" as footer text. The tokens are replaced on every page automatically.

What is N-up layout?

N-up puts 2, 4 or 6 source pages onto one sheet, so you can print handouts or save paper.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. All five tools run 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your file never leaves your device.
