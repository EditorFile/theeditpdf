How to Delete Pages from a PDF

Home › Blog › How to Delete Pages from a PDF

Short answer: To delete pages from a PDF for free, open TheEditPDF's Delete Pages tool, select your PDF, click the pages you want to remove, and download the cleaned file. Your file never leaves your device, there is nothing to install, and you do not need an account. Your original PDF stays untouched on your device, so you can always start over if you change your mind.

Almost every PDF collects a few pages you do not need: a blank page left over from a scan, a duplicated sheet, a cover page from a template, or a chapter that no longer belongs in the document. Deleting those pages makes the file cleaner, smaller and easier to share — and you do not need expensive software to do it.

With TheEditPDF's free Delete Pages tool, you remove pages right in your browser. Your file never leaves your device, there is nothing to install, and you do not need an account. The whole job usually takes less than a minute.

What you need: just the PDF file and a modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer or a phone. There is no file-size hoop to jump through for everyday documents, and no waiting for an upload to finish.

Step by step

  1. Open the Delete Pages tool — Click the button below to open the free tool. It works in any modern browser on desktop or mobile — no download and no signup.
  2. Choose your PDF — Select the PDF file from your device. The tool opens it locally in your browser, so your document is never uploaded to a server.
  3. Select the pages to remove — You will see a thumbnail for every page. Click the pages you want to delete — blank pages, duplicates, or anything you no longer need — and check the selection once before continuing.
  4. Download the new PDF — Click to build the cleaned file and download it. Your original PDF stays untouched on your device, so you can always start over if you change your mind.

Tips for a better result

  • Check page numbers first. If your PDF has printed page numbers, compare them with the thumbnails so you delete exactly the right pages.
  • Keep the original until you are sure. Download the cleaned file, open it once to confirm, and only then replace the old version.
  • Deleting pages also shrinks the file. Every removed page takes its images and text with it, so a trimmed PDF is often noticeably smaller.
  • Scanned PDFs work too. Deletion works at the page level, so it does not matter whether a page contains text or a scanned image.
  • Name the cleaned file clearly. Save it with a suffix such as “-cleaned” so you never confuse it with the original later.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting pages reduce the file size?

Yes. Each removed page takes its content with it, so the resulting PDF is proportionally smaller. For a big cut, combine this with the Compress tool.

Will my original file be changed?

No. The download is a brand-new file, and your original stays exactly as it was. If you deleted the wrong page, just run the tool again on the original.

Can I delete pages from a scanned PDF?

Yes. Page deletion does not depend on text content, so scanned pages delete just as cleanly as typed ones.

Can I delete several pages at once?

Yes. Select as many pages as you like in one pass — there is no need to repeat the process page by page.

Is it really free and private?

Yes. The tool is free with no account, and because everything runs in your browser, your file is never uploaded anywhere.

Can I delete the first or last page?

Yes — any page can go, including the cover, the final page, or a blank page at the end left over from printing.
