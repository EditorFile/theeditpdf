When is splitting in half useful?

A3 posters, spreadsheets and plans print awkwardly on home printers — cut them into A4 halves and print on ordinary paper. A4 handouts become A5 booklet pages, and wide landscape pages become two readable portrait halves for e-readers and phones.

Frequently asked questions

How does split in half work?

Each page is cut into two halves — left and right, or top and bottom. A 10-page PDF becomes a 20-page PDF, one half per page. Nothing is re-rendered, so text stays selectable.

What does A3 to double A4 mean?

An A3 page cut in half gives two A4 pages. The tool cuts A3 pages along the long edge, so each half is A4 landscape size — ready to print on A4 paper.

And A4 to double A5?

Same idea: an A4 page cut in half gives two A5 pages, handy for booklets and handouts. Pick the preset and the cut direction and target size are set for you.

Will the text still be selectable?

Yes. Halves keep the original page content — no rasterising — so text, links and vector graphics stay intact. Only the visible area changes.

Can I resize the halves to exact A4 or A5?

Yes. Choose a target size and each half is scaled (keeping its proportions, centred) to that exact page size. Without a target size, halves keep their natural half-page size.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Splitting runs entirely in your browser with pdf-lib. Your file never leaves your device.
