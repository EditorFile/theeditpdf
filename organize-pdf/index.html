Frequently asked questions

How do I reorder pages in a PDF?

Upload your PDF and drag any page thumbnail to a new position in the grid. Click Apply, then Download — the rebuilt PDF follows your new order.

Can I delete pages from a PDF?

Yes. Click the X on any page thumbnail to remove it. Deleted pages are gone from the rebuilt PDF when you click Apply.

Can I duplicate a page?

Yes. Click the duplicate (⧉) icon on a page thumbnail to insert an identical copy right after it.

Can I rotate individual pages?

Yes. Each thumbnail has rotate-left (⟲) and rotate-right (⟳) buttons. Rotations are applied when you rebuild the PDF.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser with pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your file never leaves your device.

What happens if I delete all pages?

You cannot build an empty PDF. If every page is removed, the Apply button shows a warning and nothing is downloaded — upload a new file or click Reset to restore the original order.
