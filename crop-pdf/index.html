Frequently asked questions

How do I crop a PDF online for free?

Upload your PDF to the Crop PDF tool, set the top, bottom, left and right margins (in points or percent), preview the crop area on the first page, choose which pages to crop, then click Crop PDF and download the trimmed file. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no signup.

Does cropping a PDF reduce its quality?

No. Cropping only changes the visible page area (the CropBox) — it does not recompress images or text, so the content keeps its original quality. The hidden content outside the crop area is still in the file but not displayed.

Can I crop only some pages of a PDF?

Yes. Choose All pages to crop every page, or enter a page range such as 1-3, 5, 7-9 to crop only those pages. Unselected pages keep their original size.

What is the difference between points and percent for crop margins?

Points (pt) are absolute units — 1 inch = 72 points — so a 36 pt margin is always half an inch regardless of page size. Percent margins are relative to each page's width and height, so 10% trims the same proportion on every page even if pages differ in size.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server when I crop it?

No. TheEditPDF processes files 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never leaves your device, which is why the tool works offline once loaded and needs no account.

Can I remove white borders from a scanned PDF?

Yes — that is the most common use. Set equal margins on all four sides (for example 10% or 36 pt) and use the live preview to check the result before applying. For uneven borders, adjust each side separately.
