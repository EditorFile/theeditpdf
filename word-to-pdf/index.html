Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Word to PDF for free?

Upload your .docx file, pick a page size and font size, and click Convert to PDF. The file is converted entirely in your browser and downloads as a PDF — no signup and no upload.

Does it work with .doc files?

No — only modern .docx files are supported, because the old .doc binary format cannot be read reliably in a browser. Open a .doc in Word or LibreOffice and save it as .docx first, then convert it here.

Will my formatting survive?

Headings, paragraphs, bold and italic text, lists and tables are carried over. Exact fonts, page layout and complex Word features are not reproduced pixel-for-pixel — the PDF uses clean standard fonts so the result opens everywhere.

Are images in my document kept?

PNG and JPEG images embedded in the .docx are included in the PDF. Other image formats and floating text boxes may be skipped.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser with mammoth.js and pdf-lib. Your file never leaves your device.

Can I convert several Word files at once?

Yes. Drop multiple .docx files and each one becomes its own PDF. Download them one by one or all together as a ZIP.
