When is extracting pages useful?

Extracting shines whenever only part of a document needs to go somewhere else: sending one signed page back to a client, pulling a single chapter out of a long report, isolating an invoice from a multi-invoice file, or saving one form from a scanned batch. Because pages are copied losslessly, fonts, images and vector graphics are identical to the source — nothing is re-rendered or recompressed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract pages from a PDF?

Upload your PDF, type the pages you want (for example 1-3, 5, 7-9) or click the page thumbnails to select them, then click Extract & Download. You get a new PDF containing only those pages, in document order.

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. Enter comma-separated pages and ranges, such as 1, 4-6, 9. Duplicates are removed automatically and pages are extracted in document order.

Does extracting pages reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied losslessly with pdf-lib, so fonts, images and vector content are identical to the original.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser with pdf-lib. Your file never leaves your device.

What if I type a page number that does not exist?

The tool checks your selection against the document's page count and tells you exactly which entries are out of range, so you can fix them before extracting.
