Edit PDFs directly in your browser

TheEditPDF's free PDF editor lets you change a PDF without installing software or creating an account. Click any existing text to edit it in place — the editor samples the original font, size, and color so your change blends in. You can also add brand-new text anywhere on the page, insert images, draw shapes, highlight passages, add links, fill in form fields, and place your signature. Everything happens locally in your browser, so confidential contracts, tax forms, and personal documents never leave your device.

How to edit a PDF

  1. Open the editor — it starts automatically on this page.
  2. Choose your PDF file when prompted.
  3. Click existing text to edit it, or pick a tool from the toolbar (Text, Images, Sign, Shapes, Annotate).
  4. Drag elements to reposition them and use the side panel to fine-tune formatting.
  5. Click Download PDF and choose to save, print, rename, or keep editing.

Why edit with TheEditPDF?

Most free editors stamp watermarks on your file or force a signup before downloading. TheEditPDF does neither. There are no watermarks, no accounts, and no upload queues — your file is processed on your own computer, which is also faster than waiting on a remote server. The editor works on phones and tablets too, with a touch-friendly toolbar.

Will my edits look like the original text?

Yes. When you click existing text, TheEditPDF reads the surrounding font family, size, and color from the page and applies them to your replacement automatically.

Is there a file size limit?

Because processing is local, the practical limit is your device's memory. Typical office documents of tens or hundreds of pages work fine.

1. The short version

TheEditPDF does not collect, store, or see your files. Every tool runs entirely inside your web browser, on your own device. When you choose a PDF, it is read by JavaScript running locally — it is never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate file-processing servers at all.

2. What we do not collect

  • Your documents: PDFs, images, and spreadsheets you process never leave your device. We have no way to access them.
  • Accounts: there is no signup and no login, so we hold no usernames, passwords, or profile data.
  • File contents: text you extract, signatures you draw, and redactions you make exist only in your browser tab's memory until you download the result.

3. What is collected automatically

Like almost every website, our hosting provider (Vercel) may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and reliability. We do not use analytics cookies, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting scripts of our own. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

4. Third-party libraries

TheEditPDF loads open-source JavaScript libraries (for example pdf.js and pdf-lib) from public content delivery networks (CDNs). Those CDNs may see a standard request for the library file, exactly as when any site loads a font or script. They do not receive your documents.

5. Children

TheEditPDF is a general-purpose utility not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no personal information from anyone.

6. Your control

Because files never reach us, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete on our side. Clearing your browser data removes any trace of your session from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how TheEditPDF handles information, we will update the date above and describe the change here.
